Tellya Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026. Tellya is an IPTV player developed by Ilya Baikou. It does not provide a subscription, television channels, or media rights. You choose and add your own sources.

Information handled by the app

Tellya stores your source settings (including playlist URLs, optional provider usernames and passwords, and custom HTTP headers) on your device in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. It also stores downloaded channel and programme-guide data, favorites, viewing history, playback position, and app preferences locally. Tellya has no Tellya account, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

When you add or play a source, the app connects to the playlist, guide, logo, and media servers you selected. Those providers receive the network requests necessary to deliver their content and may see your IP address and any source credentials or request headers that you supplied. Some sources use unencrypted HTTP because the source provider only supports it; choose sources you trust.

If you choose QR-code pairing, Tellya uses its pairing service to transfer the source name and URL, and, where supplied, a media URL, between your devices. A source URL can contain provider credentials. Pairing is optional; you can enter a source on the device instead. The pairing service and its hosting provider process the data needed to complete that transfer. Do not use pairing for sources you do not want to transmit to the service.

If you run connection diagnostics, Tellya may contact Google's connectivity-check endpoint and your configured source endpoints to test reachability. The app does not send viewing history to the developer.

Purpose, sharing, and retention

The data above is used to load your sources, play media, show programme information, remember your choices, and enable optional pairing. We do not sell user data or use it for advertising. Network requests are made to the source providers and, only when pairing is used, the pairing service and its hosting provider. These third parties have their own data practices.

Local data remains on your device until you remove sources, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. A pairing session has a limited lifetime; contact us if you have a question about data handled by the pairing service. Tellya does not create a user account, so there is no account to delete.

Your choices

You can avoid the optional pairing service by adding sources directly. You control which providers you connect to. To delete all data stored by Tellya on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. To ask about data or request deletion of pairing-service data, email ibaikov17@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Tellya changes. The current version will be published at this address. For privacy questions, contact ibaikov17@gmail.com.
