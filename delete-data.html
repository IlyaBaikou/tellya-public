Delete Tellya data

Tellya does not create an account. Playlist settings, guide cache, favorites, history and playback positions are stored on your device. To remove them all, clear Tellya's app storage in Android Settings → Apps → Tellya → Storage, or uninstall the app. Removing only a source in Tellya removes its saved source settings.

Request deletion of pairing-service data

If you used optional QR-code pairing and want us to check for and delete related data that remains under our control, email ibaikov17@gmail.com with the subject “Tellya data deletion request”. Include the approximate date/time of pairing and, if you still have it, the short pairing code. Do not send your IPTV password or full playlist URL. We will respond to confirm what we can identify and delete. A pairing code normally expires after about 10 minutes; data needed for operation or legal compliance, if any, may be retained for longer and we will explain that in our response.

This request does not delete data held independently by your IPTV provider or other source servers; contact those providers separately. See our privacy policy for details.
